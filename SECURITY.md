# Security / patient data and electrical safety

The NeuroEdge repo is a non-medical research prototype. There is no certified electrical isolation, stimulation output, patient front-end or implant safety analysis. Do NOT attach human electrodes to an experimental prototype relying on the illustrative SPICE netlists in this repository.

When evaluating neural data, use publicly licensed datasets and comply with consent, attribution, GDPR/privacy and institutional ethics. Do not commit raw human neural data or personal identifiers.

Report privately any exploitable software/hardware bugs involving memory-mapped control, unvalidated weight writes or malicious datasets before distributing deployment code. Do not include credentials, PDK NDAs, private chips, or company repositories in issues/logs.
